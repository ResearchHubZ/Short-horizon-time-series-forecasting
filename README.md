# Short-horizon-time-series-forecasting

## Private Datasets

This repository includes five anonymized private time series datasets collected from power and transportation systems. These datasets are used to evaluate short-horizon forecasting performance under different domains, sampling frequencies, and variable settings.

| Dataset | Domain | Time Range | Frequency | Samples | Variables | 
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| RTT | Electricity | 2024/01/01–2025/06/15 | 1 h | 12,624 | 1 | 
| REC | Electricity | 2023/01/01–2026/01/06 | 1 d | 1,102 | 4 | 
| TED | Electricity | 2025/04/01–2025/09/30 | 1 h | 4,392 | 1 | 
| DSO | Electricity | 2023/01/01–2025/08/15 | 15 min | 91,488 | 7 | 
| HTF | Traffic | 2025/05/01–2025/05/31 | 15 min | 2,880 | 46 | 


### 1. RTT Dataset

- **Data Source and Collection:** The RTT dataset is derived from real-time electricity price records from a provincial power market. The data were generated by electricity trading-related business systems and cover the period from January 1, 2024 to June 15, 2025. The dataset is recorded at an hourly frequency and contains 12,624 hourly samples, reflecting real-time electricity price changes across different periods.

- **Data Characteristics:** RTT is an hourly univariate time series dataset. The core fields include `date` and `price`, where `date` denotes the hourly timestamp and `price` denotes the real-time electricity price at the corresponding time. This dataset can reflect intra-day price fluctuations, periodic changes, seasonal variations, and abnormal market price movements.

- **Data Processing and Quality Control:** To ensure data reliability and usability, the RTT dataset was checked for time continuity, duplicate records, and abnormal values. Extreme high or low price points were marked and retained when they reflected real market fluctuations, so that abnormal price changes in the electricity market could be considered during subsequent modeling.

### 2. REC Dataset

- **Data Source and Collection:** The REC dataset records daily residential electricity consumption in a province. The data were collected from residential electricity consumption statistics of a provincial power company and were combined with publicly available temperature data from meteorological sources. The dataset covers the period from January 1, 2023 to January 6, 2026. It is recorded at a daily frequency and contains 1,102 daily samples.

- **Data Characteristics:** REC is a daily multivariate time series dataset. The core fields include `date`, `dianliang`, `temphi`, `templo`, and `avgtemp`. The field `dianliang` denotes daily residential electricity consumption and is the main forecasting target. The fields `temphi`, `templo`, and `avgtemp` represent daily maximum temperature, minimum temperature, and average temperature, respectively, which are used to describe the influence of weather conditions on residential electricity demand.

- **Data Processing and Quality Control:** To ensure data reliability and usability, the REC dataset was checked for completeness, duplicate records, and abnormal values. In addition, the temperature variables and electricity consumption records were temporally aligned, ensuring that all variables could be used under the same daily time scale for time series modeling and forecasting.

### 3. TED Dataset

- **Data Source and Collection:** The TED dataset was collected from hourly electricity consumption records of a provincial power system. The data were obtained from the electricity statistics system of a power company. The dataset covers the period from April 1, 2025 to September 30, 2025. It is recorded at an hourly frequency and contains 4,392 hourly samples.

- **Data Characteristics:** TED is an hourly univariate time series dataset. The core fields include `date` and `electricity`, where `date` denotes the hourly timestamp and `electricity` denotes the electricity consumption at the corresponding time. This dataset can reflect intra-day electricity demand patterns, short-term load fluctuations, and seasonal changes.

- **Data Processing and Quality Control:** To ensure data reliability and usability, basic preprocessing was conducted on the TED dataset. First, the raw data were checked for completeness to ensure that the time series was continuous and contained no duplicate records. Second, abnormal values were detected and verified to reduce the influence of extreme electricity consumption values on model training. Finally, the time index was standardized so that the dataset could be directly used for time series modeling and forecasting analysis.

### 4. DSO Dataset

- **Data Source and Collection:** The DSO dataset records the load rate changes of a distribution transformer in a city. The data were collected from the monitoring records of a local power company’s distribution automation system. The dataset covers the period from January 1, 2023 to August 15, 2025. It is recorded at a 15-minute frequency, with 96 samples per day.

- **Data Characteristics:** DSO is a 15-minute multivariate time series dataset. The core fields include `date`, `load_rate`, historical load statistics, `holiday`, and `weekend`. The field `load_rate` denotes the distribution transformer load rate and serves as the main forecasting target. Historical load statistics are used to capture recent periodic and trend patterns, while `holiday` and `weekend` indicate holiday and weekend information.

- **Data Processing and Quality Control:** To ensure data reliability and usability, the DSO dataset was processed through missing value checking, abnormal value detection, and temporal alignment. A small number of missing load-rate values were filled using historical statistics from the same time period. Extreme load conditions were retained after verification when they reflected real operating states, so that abnormal fluctuations in distribution transformer operation could be considered during modeling.

### 5. HTF Dataset

- **Data Source and Collection:** The HTF dataset was collected from 46 gantry-based video monitoring devices on highway sections in a province. It records traffic flow changes over time. The dataset covers the period from May 1, 2025 to May 31, 2025. It is recorded at a 15-minute frequency and contains 2,880 samples.

- **Data Characteristics:** HTF is a 15-minute traffic flow time series dataset. The core fields include `date` and 46 anonymized gantry monitoring point fields. Each monitoring point field represents the vehicle flow at the corresponding gantry within a specific 15-minute interval. This dataset can reflect intra-day traffic peak and off-peak patterns, as well as traffic flow variations under workday, weekend, and holiday conditions.

- **Data Processing and Quality Control:** To ensure data reliability and usability, the HTF dataset was checked for time continuity, duplicate records, and abnormal traffic flow values.

Due to the involvement of real power-system operation information, electricity market transaction records, and user-side electricity consumption statistics, the complete dataset is not publicly available for privacy protection and data security reasons. Sample data can be obtained by contacting the corresponding author, with usage limited to academic research and replication studies.
